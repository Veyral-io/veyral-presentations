The problem

What Atlas is

Four things, one sign-in

  • Atlas — the assistant you talk to.
  • The desk — invoices, receipts, books, calendar, to-dos, templates.
  • The shelf — what you owe, what others owe you, the morning brief.
  • The vault — the company's documents, sealed, access decided in code.
The shelf

Sessions on the left. The thing in the centre. Atlas on the right.

Sheets, workbooks, documents and tasks — each a "thing" you open, share, compare and export. Atlas writes cells, formats as kronor, opens any page, points at a cell, speaks a line.

Every company on its own store. Every live update stays inside the company — proven, not claimed.

Voice

Atlas talks while it works.

Swedish and English. A line on every action, spoken aloud, shown as a caption beside Atlas's pointer. Muted by a setting, or by the person.

How it is built

One runtime. Many products. Nothing re-implemented.

  • server — the host: doors, voice, memory, tests.
  • shelf — tasks, steps, waiting items, blocks, the approval broker.
  • receipts — invoices, OCR, seven years of keeping, booking.

Atlas is a business assistant first. No developer word on any default surface.
