The problem

A month of receipts is a hunt. Most of it is finding, not deciding.

Bank lines without documents. Documents without bank lines. A pile at the end of the month, and an accountant who has to guess.

What Novitto does

The hunt finds the document for every bank line.

  • Every receipt scanned, OCR'd and kept for seven years.
  • Every bank line matched to its document — or flagged as missing.
  • Booking suggested, you approve. Nothing is booked without you.
  • A 15-minute monthly meeting closes the month.
The month

What needs you, and what doesn't.

  • Needs you — missing receipts, unclear lines, approvals.
  • Already done — matched, booked, filed.
  • Insights — spend by month, VAT at risk, categories, what to know.
  • The meeting — a list of questions, answered together.
Connections

One place for every source.

Books, bank, mail, calendar, files, supplier portals, payments. Each a card with its state. Search across all of them.

Novitto connects to the books you already use. It does not replace them.
